# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Enterprise leaders, AI platform buyers, operations teams, and technical teams evaluating Prism Shadow as a secure AI agent platform for business workflows.

## Product Purpose

PrismShadow provides an integrated platform for data production, model training and agent self-evolution, helping enterprises build and continuously optimize AI workflows and AutoResearch systems. Products: PenguinHarness (open-source multi-agent app platform), Panofy (expert agent training) and LlamaFactory (open-model fine-tuning). The site should make the technology, results, team and customers feel world-class, stated with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Precise, luminous, and enterprise-grade. The brand should feel advanced without becoming noisy, decorative, or template-like.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS card stacks, cards inside cards, bold (600+) type, decorative gradients, glow, glassmorphism, stock or people photos, and repeated visuals that do not explain the product idea. Quality bar: modal.com, but in our own look and voice, never a copy of its layout or phrasing.

## Design Principles

- Dark technical canvas (`--ds-*` tokens in `src/index.css`), one lavender accent, spectrum colours only for small diagram highlights.
- Regular-weight type: headings 400, never above 500. Geist / Geist Mono / Noto Sans SC.
- Everything aligns to the 12-column `.ds-container` grid; hairlines instead of boxes; sparse sections, one idea and one visual each.
- Visuals are built in code (pixel fields, line/isometric schematics, flat UI mocks, charts) and explain the product. Genuine product screenshots are fine.
- Many items → tabs or list/detail, never a wall of cards. Use motion only when it supports the idea.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain AA contrast on the dark canvas and the light paper bands, one h1 per page, WAI-ARIA tabs, support reduced-motion users (final state always visible), and avoid using color alone to communicate important status. Every string exists in English and Chinese.
